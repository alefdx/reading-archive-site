Reading Archive

Reading Archive is a personal automation tool used by a single person, its developer. It is not offered to the public.

It takes documents saved to a personal Todoist project, files them into the owner's Google Drive, and records a summary of each one in the owner's Google Sheets.

It uses Google sign-in to access:

  • Google Drive, limited to the files the app itself creates (drive.file scope)
  • Google Sheets, to read and append rows in the owner's own spreadsheets
